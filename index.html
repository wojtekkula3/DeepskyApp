Deepsky App – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Deepsky App (“the app”) shows NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day and lets you keep a list of favourite pictures. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible

The app is developed and published by Wojciech Kula, a private individual based in Poland, who is the data controller for the data described below. Contact: wojtekkula33@gmail.com.

What the app does not collect

  • The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • It does not use your precise (GPS) location, contacts, camera or photos.
  • It does not show ads, does not collect the advertising ID and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

Favourites

Pictures you add to favourites are stored only in the app's local database on your device. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Analytics

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It records:

  • which screens are viewed, for example the picture of the day, the favourites list or an error screen;
  • a few actions: opening a picture full screen, opening a video and removing a picture from favourites;
  • sessions and basic technical information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • an app instance identifier generated by Firebase, which is not linked to your identity and is reset when you reinstall the app or clear its data;
  • an approximate location (country or city level) that Google derives from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored in the analytics data.

The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and fixing problems in it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Analytics data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf as a service provider; its handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is sent over an encrypted connection and may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the safeguards Google provides, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

The iOS version of the app does not currently collect any analytics.

Content from third-party servers

To show the picture of the day, the app downloads pictures, text and videos directly from NASA's servers (science.nasa.gov and related NASA domains). For video entries it may load preview images from YouTube or Vimeo, and a video you choose to open is played on that provider's site or app. Like any website you visit, these servers receive your IP address and basic request information when your device downloads their content; the developer does not receive or control that data. See the privacy policies of NASA, YouTube (Google) and Vimeo.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Because the app does not know who you are, analytics data can only be linked to you through the app instance identifier on your device; the simplest way to stop analytics and reset that identifier is to uninstall the app or clear its data. For any request or question, email wojtekkula33@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts processing data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
